How to play Yahtzee

In Yahtzee, each player gets up to three rolls per turn to build one of 13 scoring categories.

  • Roll all five dice, then choose which dice to reroll.
  • You can reroll selected dice up to two more times.
  • After rolling, choose a scoring category. Once a category is used, it cannot be chosen again.
  • Score categories include ones through sixes, three/four of a kind, full house, straights, Yahtzee, and chance.
  • The goal is to maximize your total score across 13 rounds.
